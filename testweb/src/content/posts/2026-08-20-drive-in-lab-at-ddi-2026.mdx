Drive-In Lab will participate in **DDI 2026**, the 10th International Conference
on Driver Distraction and Inattention. The conference will take place at
Lindholmen Science Park in Gothenburg, Sweden, from 20 to 22 October 2026.

DDI brings together researchers, industry specialists, and public-sector experts
working to better understand, prevent, and mitigate distraction and inattention
in traffic. Its focus closely matches our work on driver attention, the demands
of in-car information and entertainment systems, and the development of safer
driver interfaces.

Our participation will provide an opportunity to exchange ideas with the
international research community and discuss how controlled studies with real
vehicles can contribute to safer human–vehicle interaction. Drive-In Lab project
leader [Tuomo Kujala](https://www.jyu.fi/en/people/tuomo-kujala) also serves on
the [DDI 2026 Scientific Committee](https://ddi2026.org/program/committees/).

Further information about the Drive-In Lab contribution will be shared when
programme details are available.

[Visit the official DDI 2026 website](https://ddi2026.org/).
