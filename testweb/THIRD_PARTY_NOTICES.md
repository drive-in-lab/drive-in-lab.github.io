# Third-party artwork

## Perspective road

The road background in `src/assets/road-perspective.svg` is adapted and
recoloured from **“Passing Zone”** by mazeo:

- Source: https://openclipart.org/detail/93325/passing-zone
- License: CC0 / public domain
- Openclipart license information: https://openclipart.org/share

The original perspective geometry is retained. Colours and SVG metadata were
adjusted for the Drive-In Lab website.
