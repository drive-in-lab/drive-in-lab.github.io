Our research introduces methods for measuring driver inattention and the
associated crash potential in traffic, with a particular focus on how
in-car infotainment and entertainment systems draw drivers' eyes and
attention away from the road. The lab's facility allows any production
vehicle to be driven into a 6 m × 7.2 m simulation container and connected
to a realistic, controlled traffic scenario — enabling valid and reliable
measurement and rating of an OEM in-car system's distraction potential.
