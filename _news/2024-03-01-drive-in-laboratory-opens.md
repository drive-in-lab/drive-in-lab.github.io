Drive-in Lab's new simulation facility is now operational. Housed in a
6 m × 7.2 m container, the laboratory allows any production vehicle to be
driven inside and connected to a realistic driving simulation: three
projectors cast a 180° front traffic scene at 5760×1200 resolution, while
a further rear projection at 1920×1080 is visible to the driver through
the vehicle's own mirrors.

The facility enables valid and reliable measurement and rating of an
in-car infotainment system's distraction potential under controlled,
repeatable traffic conditions — supporting the group's wider research
into driver inattention and road safety.
