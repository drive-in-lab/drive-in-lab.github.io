We are excited to announce that our Drive in Lab project ** Driver Distraction and In-Vehicle Information Overload (DIVIO)** has been funded by the **Fédération Internationale de l’Automobile (FIA)**. The project aims to study driver inattention and distraction, with a focus on understanding how modern in-car information and entertainment systems affect driver attention and safety.
