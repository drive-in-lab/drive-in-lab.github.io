We are excited to announce that our Drive in Lab will be presented at the **DDI 2026** conference in Gothenburg, Sweden. The conference will take place from **Oct 20, 22, 2026**, and will bring together researchers and practitioners from around the world to discuss the latest developments in driving and driver behavior research.

Professor Kujala will hold chair for Parallel Session 3: Visual attention beyond fixation at Room Pascal from 16.00 to 17.20. also he will deliver a talk titled **"Possibility over probability: A worst-case approach to driver distraction and inattention assessment"** on **Day 3 in session 10: Safety assessment, models and validation** at the room **Tesla** from **10.20 - 11:40**.
