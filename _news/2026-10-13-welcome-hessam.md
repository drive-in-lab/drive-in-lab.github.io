We are excited to welcome **Hessam Mohseni** as a postdoctoral researcher in our Drive in Lab. Hessam has a PhD in Cognitive Science from the University of Jyväskylä, and he will be supporting our research on driver inattention and distraction. He will be working on the funded project [**Driver Distraction and In-Vehicle Information Overload (DIVO)**](/news/drive-in-lab-divio), which aims to study driver inattention and distraction, with a focus on understanding how modern in-car information and entertainment systems affect driver attention and safety.
