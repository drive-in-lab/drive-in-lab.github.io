## The Drive-in Laboratory

The lab's defining piece of infrastructure is the **Drive-in Laboratory**,
built in early 2024: a 6 m × 7.2 m container housing a full-scale driving
simulation. Any car can be driven directly into the facility and connected
to the simulation, which projects a 180° traffic scene in front of the
vehicle (three projectors, 5760×1200) and a rear scene visible through the
mirrors (1920×1080). This lets researchers run controlled, repeatable
traffic scenarios around a real production vehicle and measure how its
infotainment and driver-assistance systems affect driver attention.

## Research themes

- **Driver inattention** — modeling when and why attention lapses occur,
  and what makes certain moments more dangerous than others.
- **In-car infotainment distraction** — measuring and rating how OEM
  touchscreen and entertainment systems draw attention from driving.
- **Human-machine interaction in vehicles** — designing and evaluating
  interfaces that support, rather than compete with, safe driving.
- **Context-sensitive risk prediction** — building models that anticipate
  when inattention is likely to lead to a safety-critical event.
