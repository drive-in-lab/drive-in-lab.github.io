Aaron Khodami is a Postdoctoral Researcher at Drive-in Lab, Faculty of
Information Technology, University of Jyväskylä.

Aaron is a Postdoctoral researcher in the Cognitive Science division at the University, working with the Drive-in-lab group, which aims to understand behaviour, vision, and cognitive function while driving to advance knowledge in this area.

He holds a PhD from the University of Padua, where he completed my doctoral studies with excellence "Cum laude" and worked on vision, visual attention, eye tracking, EEG, and behavioural modelling. His Doctoral studies were funded by the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) under Horizon 2020. He has a strong passion for vision, Visual attention, modelling, mathematics in psychology, and eye tracking. He is also a Python developer, having developed a few packages, and he mostly uses it as his main analysis tool, using R when needed.
