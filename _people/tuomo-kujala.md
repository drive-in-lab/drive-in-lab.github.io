Tuomo Kujala is Professor of Cognitive Science at the Faculty of
Information Technology, University of Jyväskylä, and leads Drive-in Lab.
His research focuses on human inattention and its effects on road safety,
including how in-car information and entertainment systems affect driver
attention and crash risk. He leads the group's work with the Drive-in
Laboratory, a full-scale driving simulation facility built in 2024 for
measuring the distraction potential of in-car systems.
